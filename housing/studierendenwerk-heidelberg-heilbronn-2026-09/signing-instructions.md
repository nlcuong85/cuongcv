## 3. Room-access declaration - `Einverständniserklärung zum Betreten der Mietsache_de-engl.pdf`

Choose **exactly one** box:

- **First box - consent / `Zutrittsrecht`:** Studierendenwerk, with an accompanying representative, may enter while you are away for announced necessary maintenance or repairs. This is the practical choice if you do not want to be present for every smoke-detector or repair visit.
- **Second box - refusal / `Zutrittsverbot`:** no work while you are absent. You must arrange a personal appointment promptly after notification and could be charged for extra visits, delay-related damage, or enforcement costs.

For a student who may be at university or work during the day, the **first box is usually the simpler choice**. You can revoke this consent later in writing.

Then fill:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| `Ort, Datum` | `Heilbronn,` followed by the date you sign. |
| `Unterschrift` | Your signature. |

Save this one signed page as a separate PDF.
