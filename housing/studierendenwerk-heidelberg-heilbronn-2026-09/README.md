## Key choices

- The room-access declaration is revocable in writing. Consent allows announced maintenance/repairs to be performed during absence, accompanied by a Studierendenwerk representative; refusal can create extra-cost risk when access is needed.
- The SEPA mandate is required for the contract and permits rent, charges, and the deposit to be collected. Only euro-area bank accounts are accepted.
- The house rules prohibit, among other things, corridor/stairwell parties, open flames, smoke-detector tampering, and unapproved network changes. Overnight visitors are restricted.
