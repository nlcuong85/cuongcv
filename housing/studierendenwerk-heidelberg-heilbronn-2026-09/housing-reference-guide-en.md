## What the important terms mean in everyday language

### Fixed contract length

The room is reserved until 31 August 2028. It is not an open-ended rental agreement. Being a student is necessary for the accommodation, but it does not automatically extend the lease after that date.

### Rent, admission fee, and deposit

- **Rent:** EUR 370.00 per month.
- **Admission fee:** EUR 30.00 one-time charge with the first rent.
- **Deposit:** EUR 650.00, collected after the contract begins.
- **Direct debit:** the rent and permitted charges are collected from the bank account authorised in the SEPA mandate.

### Room access for maintenance

The room-access declaration concerns announced maintenance or repairs, such as smoke-detector checks or electrical work. Emergency access remains possible where necessary to protect people or prevent damage.

### Dormitory rules

This is shared student housing. The core practical rules are:

- respect other residents, especially study and quiet time;
- do not hold parties in corridors or stairwells;
- do not use open flames or interfere with smoke detectors;
- keep corridors and escape routes clear;
- do not smoke in shared rooms;
- keep shared kitchens, bathrooms, and waste areas clean;
- do not make unauthorised changes to the building or network;
- use the internet legally and securely.
