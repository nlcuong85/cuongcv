# AGENTS.md - Studierendenwerk Heidelberg accommodation record

## Purpose

This folder is the private local record for Le Cuong Nguyen's student-accommodation offer from Studierendenwerk Heidelberg for Heilbronn.

## Folder layout

- `received/` contains unmodified source PDFs received with the offer.
- `submitted/` is reserved for the exact signed PDFs and sent-email evidence after submission.
- `submission-log.md` records the current return-package status, drafts, and outstanding documents.
- `housing-reference-guide-en.md` is the EPUB-ready English explanation of the complete housing package.
- `README.md` is the plain-language checklist and summary.
- `CHANGELOG.md` records every addition, replacement, or submission-related update.

## Handling rules

- Preserve files in `received/`; do not overwrite, annotate, sign, or otherwise modify them.
- Before creating a signed version, verify the document, page count, signature field, and email-file-size requirement.
- Store only the exact final PDFs that were sent in `submitted/`, retaining the original filename or a clear date suffix.
- Do not store passwords, PIN letters, bank-account numbers, or unnecessary identity-document copies here.
- Treat the rental agreement, Studierendenwerk instructions, and signed documents as authoritative over any local summary.
- Keep the current certificate of enrollment separate until it is actually needed; record its submission in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Required return package

The offer email requires the signed rental agreement, SEPA mandate, information sheet, and room-access consent to be emailed as one PDF per page, each under 5 MB, by 22 July 2026. A separate `Hinweiszettel` / `Hinweis Wohnzeitverlängerung` should be obtained if it has not been supplied.
